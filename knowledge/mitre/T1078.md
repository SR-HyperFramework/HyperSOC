# MITRE T1078 — Valid Accounts

## Metadata

- source: MITRE
- technique: T1078
- category: defense-evasion

Valid accounts may be abused to access systems without creating a new identity.
Successful authentication after repeated failures can be relevant evidence, but
analysts should confirm the user, source, time, and expected access pattern.

## Investigation

Review login history, MFA or authentication method, source IP, host role, and
recent account changes. Do not infer compromise from a single successful login.
